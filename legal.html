Privacy notice

In short: the maintainer collects nothing. The app has no server, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

  • On your device. Your materials, highlights, ink, notes, calendar, settings and training results are stored in your browser's own storage (local storage and IndexedDB) on your device. They are not sent to the maintainer. You can delete them at any time by removing items in the app or clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Optional Google Drive sync. If you choose "Sign in to sync", Google asks for your permission. The app then requests only the drive.appdata permission, which lets it read and write a hidden folder that belongs to this app in your own Google Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files. Data travels directly between your browser and Google; the maintainer never receives it. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the stored data from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.
  • Google API Services. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That data is used only to sync your own study data between your devices. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not read by any person.
  • Read aloud. Listen mode uses the voices provided by your browser and operating system. Some voices (for example Microsoft Edge "Natural" voices and Google voices in Chrome) are produced online by Microsoft or Google from the text being read, under their own privacy terms. Voices installed on your device work offline.
  • Hosting. The app is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts and file readers are served from the same site; no other third-party content loads unless you sign in to sync.
  • Your rights. Because the maintainer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You control all of your data directly as described above. This applies under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR and similar laws.
  • Children. The app is meant for university and postgraduate students and is not directed at children.
  • Contact. Questions about this notice: open an issue on the app's GitHub repository (github.com/abdulumar04).
